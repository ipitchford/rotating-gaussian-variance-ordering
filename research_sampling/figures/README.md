# Figure: variance landscape

`variance_landscape.pdf` is a vector publication figure; `variance_landscape.png` is a 220 dpi preview. Both are generated by `../code/verify.py` from analytic formulas. The colours are plum, ochre, teal and terracotta.

**Suggested caption.** Rotation and variance in the matched Gaussian sampler. **A:** The normalised continuous-time asymptotic variance of a linear observable, for friction below, at and above the sharp threshold. **B:** The normalised variances of unit-norm angular Hermite modes of degrees 1, 2, 4 and 6, each with angular frequency equal to its degree, at friction 2. **C:** At friction 2 and observation horizon 1, the exact variance of the time average increases between rotation strengths 2π and 3π, although the continuous-time asymptotic variance decreases. The vertical axis is logarithmic. **D:** Regular observation at interval 1 produces a periodic variance curve through exact aliasing. Independent Poisson observation at rate 1 preserves the continuous-time ordering, with an added variance floor of 1 for this unit-variance observable. Panels C and D use the first position coordinate. All initial distributions are stationary and all parameters are dimensionless.

These curves illustrate proved formulas. The plots do not establish universal monotonicity or numerical-integrator performance.
